Martin Splitt is a photographer specialising in **landscapes**, **aerial**, **underwater**, **night**, and **architecture photography**. 

Capturing moments where light transforms nature and space—from the depths of sub-aquatic wrecks and thermal caves to vibrant cityscapes and panoramic vistas from high above—Martin's work emphasizes atmospheric depth, raw emotion, and structural elegance.

His photographs have been featured in juried exhibitions across Zurich, Vienna, and New York, curated and awarded on **1x.com**, and recognized internationally in the **Top Photo Awards (TPA)**.

### Specialized Domains

* **Night & Light**: Documenting how light shapes, transforms, and illuminates urban and natural environments after dark.
* **Aerial Perspective**: Capturing geometry, textures, and unseen horizons from above using state-of-the-art aerial gear.
* **Underwater & Cave Photography**: Exploration of marine wildlife, historical shipwrecks, and complex thermal cave systems under strict technical parameters.
* **Landscape & Architecture**: Fine-art documentation of pristine landscapes and striking architectural monuments around the globe.
